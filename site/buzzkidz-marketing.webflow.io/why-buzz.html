Why we buzz

Buzz Programmes are custom-designed, internationally accredited, and proven to nurture and grow children's natural creativity and other important 21st Century Skills!

Our Story

Launched by Managing Director, Hanneke Mackie, in 2012, Buzz interactive workshops are custom designed to develop children's confidence, boost their social skills and offer them a platform to express their individuality.

Since 2012, Buzz has earned an excellent reputation for creating original children's content and delivering educational creative arts across South Africa. Buzz content includes music, podcasts and television programmes for children. The Buzz Foundation was established to operate all of the same services for underserved communities.

Our Mission

Why we do what we do...

Our Thesis

Educational reform is urgent! With the rise of Artificial Intelligence, the "future of work" calls for educational outcomes to include 21st Century skills development like creativity.

Inclusivity

All children benefit from exposure to the arts. Those who do not learn in traditional ways are often marginalised and stigmatised. All children, including those with alternative learning styles and neurodiversity, thrive in a learning environment that uses the entire body and play.

Compliance

The Department of Basic Education in South Africa mandates that learners must engage with Creative Arts Curricula (CAPS). They must be afforded the opportunity to engage with everyday learning topics via the performing arts.

Curriculum-Aligned

Buzz Fills a Gap in the Education Sector

The South African Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement (CAPS) document dictates that throughout the Life Skills subject children must be exposed to and engage with visual art, dance, drama and music in order to develop as creative and imaginative individuals, and they must be allowed the opportunity to creatively communicate, dramatise, sing, make music, dance and explore movement. Buzz fills this gap and provides this solution to the education sector.
