Our Curriculum

How we buzz

Buzz has created original curricula and learning material for each developmental stage of childhood from birth to 13 years old. Thandi, Ben and Zadie are our 3 central Buzz characters. This trio of friends go on incredible adventures. Each adventure takes place in an imaginary world with its own characters, songs and stories.

What Happens in a Buzz Workshop?

Regardless of a child's brand stage or our Buzz theme, we buzz like this...

Dance

Boy & girl-friendly, age appropriate, fun dance choreography to original Buzz tracks. High energy physical games and exercises.

Drama

Children are led on a themed, interactive adventure through improvisation and character play with props and costume.

Singing

Modern, age appropriate, original Buzz songs, raps and poems. Fun, challenging, cognitive games and exercises.

Our original content

Meet Thandi, Ben & Zadie

Thandi

"Puff your chest. Try your best. Ace the test!"

Thandi's big, beautiful hair mirrors her sassy, strong spirit just perfectly. Six going on sixteen, she sits comfortably in the driver's seat of this Buzz trio.

Ben

"We must stop at the explanation station!"

The world is full of puzzles that are begging to be solved. Thank goodness for the brilliant, brainy Ben – the perpetually pondering 5-and-a-half-year-old boy of the trio.

Zadie

"Gimme it..... NOW!..... please:)"

Zany Zadie is a fierce little firecracker – dynamite does come in small Zadie-sized packages. Youngest of the three, her limitless energy is a crucial ingredient to their success.

8 Adventure Themes

Dinosaurs

Meet Trixie the Triceratops and friendly dinosaurs, returning a mamma plesiosaur's egg. Teaches the prehistoric world.

Fairytale Forest

Jack invites fairytale friends to his beanstalk birthday party, but the Witch cuts the beanstalk down.

Around the World

The Buzz Mobile travels the world collecting ingredients for Chef Soufflé and Phileas Fogg, teaching cultures, customs and traditional foods.

Under the Sea

Help Shelley the Shark find the Whale Queen's lost Pearl of the Ocean before the Electric Eels get it.

Mermaids and Pirates

An island adventure to Treasure Island to find the Magical Coconut, meeting Patrick the Pirate.

Superheroes

Join Buzz Superheroes Bounce, Blast and more as they defend Planet Hero.

Outer Space

They meet Scotty the Spaceman, on a mission to keep the galaxies safe from villain Galliumina and her Gas-o-Thron 3000. Teaches the solar system and global warming.

African Safari

Thandi, Ben and Zadie meet Jabulani the Giraffe and Ella the Elephant on their quest to save Rafiki the Rhino.
