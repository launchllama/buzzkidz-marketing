Buzz Kidz Podcast

Buzz makes original story-based podcasts for kidz!

Listen to the audio stories of Thandi, Ben and Zadie who go on different adventures in a variety of Buzz imaginary worlds. Listening to podcasts asks of little people to shut off some of their senses, escape into their imaginations and ‘make’ the picture to fit the story in their minds. A calming, soothing, gentle activity, great for your child’s development.

Buzz Edutainment TV Show

Buzz has created a revolutionary new edutainment television show for children. The synopsis: Entertaining presenters, dancers and singers break the fourth wall, into camera, inviting kidz to buzz along at home. In each jam-packed, adventure-episode, age-appropriate drama, dance and singing elements are used to entertain and educate.

The creative arts are blended with fun, educational games and activities rooted in child development and early childhood education concepts. Colourful learn-along music videos to original music are scattered through each episode.

Thandi, Ben and Zadie are animated characters who come to life during each episode’s storytime. Each season their adventures take place in a unique imaginary world, mirroring the presenters’ setting, with its own characters, adventures and challenges. It is a multi-media format which includes live action and 3D animation in HD.
