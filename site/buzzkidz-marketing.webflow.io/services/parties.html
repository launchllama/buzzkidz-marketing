Buzz Parties

Let your child’s birthday party buzz with our themed interactive birthday adventures!

How we do it...

Buzz Kidz are party entertainment specialists. We offer Themed Birthday Adventure Parties for children aged 3 to 9 years.

We arrive at the place and time of your choice, and our Buzz Kidz Party Facilitators transform into characters from your chosen theme. Our professional, highly trained and energetic facilitators lead the birthday child and their friends on an interactive imagination adventure built around the theme.

The high-energy experience includes games, music, movement and character play. Because children naturally suspend disbelief, they quickly become immersed in the imaginary story and love helping the characters save the day.

Unlike many party entertainers, Buzz Kidz is not a show. Children are not sitting and watching, they are up on their feet, actively participating throughout the party. Expect howls of laughter, cheers of excitement and real joy as children play, pretend and become part of the story.

While the children are fully engaged in their adventure, parents can relax and enjoy time with their guests.
