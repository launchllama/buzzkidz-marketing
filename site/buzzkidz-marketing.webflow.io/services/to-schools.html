In-School Programmes

Buzz for Schools

3-in-1 Drama, Dance and Singing workshops delivered in-school, custom-developed for each cognitive developmental phase of childhood, plus Professional Development courses for teachers. Every session is CAPS-aligned to the Life Skills curriculum.

We offer 4 different services in schools

Buzz is designed for children from birth to 13 years old, with age-differentiated content and original material for every developmental stage. Learners progress through a structured set of 18 Learning Outcomes that build creativity, communication, imagination, motor development, confidence and collaboration.

Buzz in the Academic Day

Drama, dance and singing workshops delivered inside the academic day, a turnkey Performing Arts Department for your school.

Our programme strengthens whole-child development and aligns seamlessly with all major curriculum frameworks, including: CAPS & ELDAs (DBE), Cambridge, International Baccalaureate PYP, Montessori, Reggio Emilia, IEB.

We also support schools with concert creation, using original music, stories and choreography loved by children and teachers everywhere.

Buzz Kidz Extramurals

A dynamic, high-energy, 3-in-1 drama, dance and singing experience exclusively for your school community. It’s fun, interactive and value-for-money, while boosting essential 21st-century skills such as creativity, communication, confidence and teamwork.

Buzz manages everything: enquiries, registrations, invoicing, assessments, reporting, and parent communication. A zero-admin, high-impact addition to your school’s extramural schedule!

License to Buzz

Schools may acquire a licence to access our full Buzz Method, original learning material and comprehensive, age-aligned curricula, delivered through our online platform, Buzz-in-a-Box.

It is both a professional development tool and a creative curriculum solution, enabling schools to embed structured drama, dance and music into everyday teaching, without requiring any prior arts training.

Bring Learning Alive

“Bring Learning Alive” is a 2-hour, high-energy training experience for Preschool, ECD and Foundation Phase teachers.

Using the teacher’s own voice, body and presence as creative tools, we share practical, ready-to-use methods that make learning joyful, embodied, expressive and memorable.
